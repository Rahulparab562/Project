💌 A Letter For You

To My Birthday Girl ❤️, Happy Birthday, my love. It's funny how life works sometimes. If someone had told me a year ago that the girl who was my friend,would one day become the person I look forward to the most, I probably wouldn't have believed them. But here we are. What makes us special to me isn't that we suddenly fell in love. We laughed as friends. We shared random updates. We became apart of each other's routines without even realizing it. And then somewhere between all those conversations, all those little moments, all those "have you eaten?" texts, all those calls, and all those days of simply being there for each other... you became so much more. One of the things I admire most about you is your heart. The way you care. The way you worry. The way you love. Sometimes you feel too much, sometimes you overthink, sometimes you cry, but those are also the things that make you beautifully you. You've trusted me with parts of yourself that weren't easy to share. You've shown me a kind of trust that still leaves me in awe. We've had our imperfect moments too. Days when we misunderstood each other. Days when one of us was upset. Days when we had to choose understanding over ego. And somehow, every single time, we found our way back to each other. I think that's one of my favorite things about us. Not that we never struggle. But that we choose each other even when we do. Today, on your birthday, I don't want to make huge promises. I just want to promise you this: I will continue choosing us. I will continue learning you. I will continue being honest with you. And I will continue appreciating the beautiful soul who somehow turned from my best friend into one of the most important people in my life. Thank you for your patience. Thank you for your care. Thank you for every update, every call, every smile, every effort. Thank you for being you. I hope this year brings you peace when you need it, strength when you doubt yourself, happiness that stays, and all the love you deserve. And selfishly... I hope I get to be there for a lot of it. Happy Birthday, bebo❤️ With all my love, Rahul
